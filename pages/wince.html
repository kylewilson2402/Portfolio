CH 11Brand identity · Packaging · Brand guidelines

WINCE

A full brand for the sourest candy in the world, built twice. Two logo systems, two 27 page brand guides, and every touch point from the pouch to the Instagram ad.

Version 01 · The Face ↓Version 02 · The Squeeze ↓

WINCE makes sour hard candy that pushes every flavor past the point of comfort. Each piece is coated in acid, wrapped on its own and sold in a 5 oz resealable pouch, and every flavor is rated on the Wince Meter so you know what you are getting into. The audience is teens and young adults who film everything, so the brand talks like your friend daring you to do something dumb: “You’ll make the face.” “Level 5? Good luck.”

The wordmark is built from Anton and warped so the word pinches in the middle like it is wincing, with acid dripping off the bottom of the letters. The palette is Pitch Black and Lemon Shock with three flavor colors, Apple Acid, Razz Blast and Sour Gold, and the type pairs Anton headlines with Space Grotesk.

I took the identity through two versions. Version 01 is built around the TOO SOUR face mark: a real reaction photo, traced into four flat tones, with a censor bar across it because the face is too sour to show. Version 02 swaps the face for the Squeeze mark: a fist crushing a lemon with real juice running down the hand and breaking out of the ring, and the TOO SOUR bar becomes a warning label. Both versions have their own brand guide below, so you can flip through them side by side.
