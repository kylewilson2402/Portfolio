Design Principles

A typographic quote book. Seven design principles, each one acted out by a quote from Maya Angelou, Martin Luther King Jr., Nelson Mandela or Winston Churchill.

See the quotes ↓

Design Principles is a typographic book that explores seven principles of design through the words of Maya Angelou, Martin Luther King Jr., Nelson Mandela, and Winston Churchill.

Each quote is its own panel and each panel acts out the principle it's named after. Repetition literally repeats "Nothing will work" until it lands on "unless you do." Contrast builds a mountain of the word despair and drops a single stone of hope under it. Symmetry splits Mandela's line down a center rule. Rhythm turns Churchill's never give in into a beat you can hear.

The panels are type only, black and white, built in Illustrator. The cover is done in Photoshop with gold type over the quotes, and the whole thing is laid out in InDesign as a saddle stitched booklet.
