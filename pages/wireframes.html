CH 07UX · Figma · Prototype

Wireframes

UX flows and page layouts from Figma. The full Wilson Coffee Beans mobile checkout flow and the desktop layout for the Manchester United Hub.

Open the Figma prototype ↗

Wireframes come before any code gets written. This is where I map out the user experience, figure out what goes where, and test the flow before it's real.

The Wilson Coffee Beans wireframe is a full mobile ecommerce flow: homepage, product detail, checkout with a payment form, and order confirmation, all designed and prototyped in Figma so I could test it like a real app.

The Manchester United Hub desktop wireframe is the 1440 × 900 layout I blocked out in Figma before building the live site: nav, hero, stats bar, club overview and the route into each section.

I do this as a standalone service or as part of a larger web project, along with brand guides, social media kits and pitch decks.
