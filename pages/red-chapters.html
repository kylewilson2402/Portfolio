Red Chapters

A full Manchester United fan magazine. Seventeen pages, cover to back cover, and you can flip through every one of them below.

Flip through it ↓

Red Chapters is a Manchester United fan magazine with the tagline The Voice of Old Trafford. Vol. 01 is The Season Review: the collapse of 2024/25, Casemiro's long goodbye, Carrick's miracle run, and a captain who broke a record that stood for twenty three years.

It's built like a real issue. A full bleed photo cover, a contents page, an editor's letter, big photo openers for each feature, stat spreads, a tactics page with the formation drawn out, a "by the numbers" page and a back cover teasing Vol. 02. Cream paper pages and dark pages swap back and forth so the pacing never gets flat.

The type does a lot of the work. Heavy condensed headlines, United red for the numbers that matter, and big pull quotes that stop you between columns.
