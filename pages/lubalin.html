CH 03Editorial · InDesign · MMA 100

Lubalin

Layout and cover for a four page magazine on Herb Lubalin and the letter as image. My final project for MMA 100.

Flip through it ↓

A four page magazine about Herb Lubalin, the designer who treated letters as images. I went with a magazine layout because Lubalin basically defined what American magazines looked like in the 60s and 70s, so doing a magazine about him felt like the right move instead of just dropping his work on a plain page.

The page is set on six columns and ten rows. On the cover his portrait runs almost full bleed because he was the whole story, with just the masthead and the tagline on top. The spread on pages 2 and 3 uses two and three column text blocks so the article reads like an actual feature, with the script type photo on the left page and the big work collage across the top of the right page. The back flips to black so the Works page frames his logos like they're being archived.

A bold serif carries the headlines and the pull quote, a clean serif handles the body, and a geometric sans close to Avant Garde Gothic (the typeface Lubalin actually designed) runs the masthead, labels and captions. The white inside pages against the dark cover and black back page create a sandwich that makes the article feel like the middle of a real issue.
