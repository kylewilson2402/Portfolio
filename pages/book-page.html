CH 06Print · Photoshop · Blackletter

From the Yard to the City

A Photoshop book page about my journey from Jamaica to New York, set in Blackletter like an illuminated manuscript.

A single 8.5 × 11 book page about me, designed in Photoshop. The theme is my journey from Jamaica to New York, From the Yard to the City.

The brief called for Blackletter body copy, a two paragraph bio, graphic symbols, and clear hierarchy, legibility and contrast. I set the big name and the illuminated initials in Blackletter (UnifrakturMaguntia and Pirata One), used the outline of Jamaica filled with the flag as the main graphic symbol, and split the bio into two columns like a manuscript page.

The final version keeps it clean on parchment with red rules. The alternate direction pushes it the other way, with the city skyline and the flag behind a framed bio panel.
