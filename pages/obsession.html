Obsession

A horror poster redesign for Curry Barker's Obsession. Red roses, a figure in the doorway, and a title you can see a face through.

A poster redesign for Obsession, the horror film written and directed by Curry Barker. The film is about a wish that can't be taken back, so the poster is built around that: something sweet on the surface with something wrong underneath.

A bouquet of dark red roses fills the middle of the frame while a silhouette waits in the lit doorway behind it. The title runs across the bottom in a tall, sharp serif, with a frame from the film showing through the letters so the face is literally trapped inside the word OBSESSION. The tagline, He wished she would love him more than anyone., gets its answer in red at the top: SHE DOES.

The palette stays dark and warm, with lamp light, blood red and a cold teal in the title, and the billing block and the line One wish. No taking it back. close it out like a real theatrical one sheet.
